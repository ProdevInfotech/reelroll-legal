Privacy Policy
Application: ReelRoll (com.prodevinfotech.reelroll)
Developer: Prodev Infotech
Effective Date: October 7, 2026

100% On-Device Processing: ReelRoll does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your photos, videos, or personal data on remote servers. All media stays exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Access

ReelRoll operates solely as a local media viewer and reel maker. It accesses the following items locally on your device:

  • Photos & Videos: Read from your device's MediaStore to display media, create reels, and organize albums.
  • Media Location & EXIF: Reads camera details and GPS coordinates embedded in photos solely to resolve city names on your device using Android's native Geocoder. Location data is never sent off-device.
  • Microphone: Voice-over recordings you make in the editor are stored only on your device and embedded in your exported video.
  • Exported Reels: Videos and audio you export are saved to Movies/ReelRoll and Music/ReelRoll on your device.
  • Collections & Tags: Custom hashtags and albums you create are stored strictly in your device's local application storage.

2. Permissions Requested

The app requests only the permissions strictly necessary for its core gallery functionality:

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
Displaying local images and generating photo reel animations.
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
Playing and looping local videos in the vertical feed.
READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED
Supporting partial photo selection on Android 14+.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Reading photos and videos on older Android versions (≤ Android 12).
ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION
Reading embedded photo GPS tags without OS redaction to show local location badges.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
Reading local audio for background music and beat-synced reels.
RECORD_AUDIO
Recording voice-over commentary in the editor. Recordings stay on your device and are only used in your export.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Showing video export progress and completion status.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
Keeping video export and preview generation running reliably while the app is in the background.
INTERNET
Checking for official app updates via Google Play In-App Updates.

3. Data Collection, Sharing & Third Parties

  • Data Collection: We do not collect or upload personal data. No accounts or registrations exist.
  • Data Sharing: We do not share your data with any third parties or advertisers.
  • Third-Party SDKs: The app does not include any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. It uses only the official Google Play In-App Update API (com.google.android.play:app-update) to inform users of app updates.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored on your device:

  • You can delete all application data (tags, collections, favorites) at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings → Apps → ReelRoll → Clear Storage or by uninstalling the app.
  • Uninstalling the app will not delete your original photos or videos from your phone.

5. Children's Privacy

ReelRoll is a general-audience tool that does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
